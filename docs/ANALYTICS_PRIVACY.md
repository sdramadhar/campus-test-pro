# Analytics Privacy

Analytics is role-aware and tenant-isolated. Super Admin platform metrics avoid unnecessary student-level detail. College Admin users are scoped to their own college. Faculty analytics are constrained to assigned subjects and batches. Student analytics expose only the signed-in student's published results and allowed ranking data.

Predictive analytics are disabled by default. Phase 14 provides a transparent rule-based foundation only, with minimum data thresholds, confidence warnings, and an explicit ban on punitive or high-stakes automated action.

AI insights use aggregate payloads only and are labelled as suggestions requiring human review.
